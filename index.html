Home Assistant Nest

A personal, single-household integration connecting the operator's own Google Nest devices to a privately operated Home Assistant instance.

What it does

Uses Google's Smart Device Management API and Cloud Pub/Sub to make authorised thermostat readings and controls, camera streams, and supported motion and doorbell events available in Home Assistant. Access is limited to the devices and permissions approved by the Google account holder.
